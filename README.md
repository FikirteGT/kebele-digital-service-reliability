# Factors Affecting the Reliability of Digital Public Services and Customer Satisfaction: A Study of Kebele Service Delivery

## 1. Project Overview

This research investigates the technical, human, and organizational factors affecting the reliability of digital public services in a selected kebele office in Ethiopia.

It also examines the relationship between digital service reliability and customer satisfaction, particularly how system interruptions, waiting times, repeated visits, and problem-handling practices affect citizens' experiences.

The study uses a **mixed-method approach**, combining quantitative and qualitative data collected through questionnaires, semi-structured interviews, and non-participant observation.

### Research Objectives

- Identify the technical factors affecting digital service reliability.
- Assess employee digital skills, technical training, and support mechanisms.
- Examine citizens' satisfaction with kebele digital services.
- Analyze the relationship between digital service reliability and customer satisfaction.
- Provide recommendations for improving digital public service delivery.

## 5. Datasets

The study will use both primary and secondary data.

### Primary Data

- Questionnaire responses from approximately 20–30 digital public service users.
- Semi-structured interviews with approximately 5–8 employees.
- Non-participant observation records, where permission is granted.

### Secondary Data

- Relevant scholarly articles and previous research studies.
- Available institutional documents, such as service procedures and maintenance records, subject to permission and access.

Collected research data will be organized and anonymized before being included in the repository.

## 6. Research Methodology

- **Research Approach:** Mixed-method approach.
- **Research Design:** Cross-sectional research design.
- **Sampling Techniques:** Convenience sampling for service users and purposive sampling for employees.
- **Data Collection Methods:** Questionnaires, semi-structured interviews, and non-participant observation.

## 7. Analysis and Reproduction Instructions

The study will use the following analysis methods:

### Quantitative Analysis

- Frequencies and percentages.
- Means where appropriate.
- Correlation analysis, where the collected data and sample size permit.

### Qualitative Analysis

- Reviewing interview transcripts and observation notes.
- Coding collected information.
- Grouping codes into relevant themes.
- Identifying patterns related to technical, human, and organizational factors.

### Reproducing the Analysis

The instructions for reproducing the analysis will be added after data collection and analysis are completed.

They will include:

1. Accessing the anonymized research datasets in the `data/` folder.
2. Identifying the software and tools required.
3. Following the analysis procedures documented in the `analysis/` folder.
4. Comparing the reproduced results with the reported findings.
